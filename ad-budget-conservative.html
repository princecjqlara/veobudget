The daily rules

ProfitUp 1 measured step
Break-evenDown 1 step
LossDown 2 compounded steps

Smaller base steps taper sooner as the budget grows. On Profit, modest team and campaign-day boosts allow gradual growth, while a 20% default reserve protects capacity. Today's budget remains the base for tomorrow. The sales quota rounds budget ÷ CPA up to whole projects.
