The daily rules

ProfitUp 1 aggressive step
Break-evenDown 1 step
LossDown 2 compounded steps

The base percentage step gets smaller at higher budgets. On Profit, a later campaign day raises the step and a larger team unlocks growth from unused capacity. Today's budget remains the base for tomorrow. The sales quota rounds budget ÷ CPA up to whole projects.
